About the Rankings

The NFL power rankings are a weekly assessment of the best teams in the league based on their performance, statistics, and overall strength. These rankings are updated every week to reflect the latest games and outcomes.

What do the rankings represent?

The rankings show the strength of each team based on win/loss ratio and strength of schedule.
